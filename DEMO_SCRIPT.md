# SALVERA — 90-Second Demonstration Script

Follow this structured presentation flow to showcase SALVERA during technical interviews, portfolio walkthroughs, or video demonstrations.

---

### Step 1: Motivation & Visual Identity (0:00 - 0:20)
1. Launch the platform using `run.bat` and open `http://localhost:5173`.
2. **Highlight the Editorial Design:** Point out the full-width workspace, restrained color palette (`#F6F7F5` surface, `#286B59` primary forest accent), high-density typography, and **absence of a left sidebar** in favor of horizontal top navigation.
3. Show the **Persistent Synthetic Benchmark Notice** at the top: explain how SALVERA transparently distinguishes *observed market data*, *statistical associations*, *ML predictions*, and *user offer inputs*.

### Step 2: Overview & Salary Explorer (0:20 - 0:40)
1. On the **Market Overview** screen, point out the calculated metrics: 2,250 records, 21 roles, 85 skills, 7 Indian tech hubs, and median annual compensation (₹24.2 LPA).
2. Show the **Salary Distribution Histogram** and **Experience Progression Curve** (+42% mid-level inflection).
3. Navigate to **Salary Explorer**: filter by `Data Scientist` in `Bengaluru`.
4. Point out the live updated quartile metrics (P25, Median, P75), the experience scatter plot, and click **Export CSV** to demonstrate instant data download.

### Step 3: Skill Intelligence & Multi-Profile Comparison (0:40 - 0:55)
1. Click **Skill Intelligence**: showcase the ranked technical skills leaderboard.
2. Demonstrate the **Multi-Profile Skill Comparison Studio**:
   - Compare `Profile A: [Python]` vs `Profile B: [Python, SQL, AWS]`.
   - Point out the delta in matching records and median salary.
3. Highlight the **Statistical Association Caveat**: explain why SALVERA refuses to claim false causality and instead provides empirical itemset lift metrics.

### Step 4: Offer Comparison Studio (0:55 - 1:10)
1. Switch to **Offer Studio**: show the side-by-side evaluation of two tech offers (e.g. ZetaScale Tech vs NexaFin Solutions).
2. Explain the **Decomposition Engine**:
   - Stated CTC includes one-time joining bonuses and perks.
   - Recurring Gross excludes one-time payments.
   - Monthly Take-Home calculates cash in-hand after statutory deductions.
   - **Net Disposable Surplus** subtracts real city living costs (rent, commute) to show true purchasing power.
3. Click **Export Official PDF**: open the generated ReportLab document showing the formatted executive compensation review.

### Step 5: Machine Learning Salary Estimator & Career Planner (1:10 - 1:30)
1. Navigate to **Salary Estimator**: enter `Machine Learning Engineer`, `4.0 years`, `Bengaluru`, and select `[Python, SQL, PyTorch, MLOps]`.
2. Click **Calculate Model Prediction**:
   - Show the predicted salary (e.g. ₹28.50 LPA).
   - Point out the **90% Confidence Interval** (lower bound to upper bound).
   - Review the **Feature Importance Weighting** (showing how each factor contributed).
   - Show the **Model Evaluation Benchmark Table** comparing Linear Regression, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting (R² = 85.5%).
3. Switch to **Career Planner**: showcase the automated skill gap roadmap from `Junior Analyst` to `Machine Learning Engineer`, toggle a milestone to "Completed", and download the personalized **Career Plan PDF**.
